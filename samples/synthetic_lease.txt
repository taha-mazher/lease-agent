RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES. This Lease is made between Marina Crest Holdings W.L.L. (the "Landlord") and Mr. Omar Haddad (the "Tenant").

2. PREMISES. The Landlord lets to the Tenant Apartment 1204, Tower B, Marina Crest Residences, Lusail Marina District, Doha, together with parking bay B-77.

3. TERM. The Lease is for a fixed term of twenty-four (24) months, commencing on 1 March 2026 and expiring on 31 March 2028.

4. RENT. The monthly rent is QAR 9,500 (nine thousand five hundred Qatari Riyals), payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month. The annual rent is QAR 114,000.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. On signing, the Tenant shall pay a security deposit of QAR 9,000, refundable at the end of the term less any sums lawfully deducted.

6. RENT ESCALATION. The rent may be increased upon renewal as mutually agreed between the parties.

7. RENEWAL. The Tenant may renew this Lease for a further twelve (12) months by giving the Landlord at least sixty (60) days written notice before the expiry date.

8. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Lease by giving two (2) months written notice. A Tenant who terminates before the end of the fixed term shall pay a penalty equal to two months of rent.

9. MAINTENANCE. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and for the air conditioning units and water heater. The Tenant is responsible for minor repairs and for damage caused by misuse.
